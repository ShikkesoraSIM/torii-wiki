# FAQ

Short answers to the questions that come up most. Each one links to the full version.

## Why did my score give 0pp?

Usually one of three things: a Relax/Autopilot score under 75% accuracy, a Flashlight score with non-default settings, or a mod that does not earn pp. Full list on the [Scoring & pp](/wiki/Scoring) page. The score still submitted and still ranks by total score, it just gave no pp.

## My friends (or country) leaderboard is empty. Is it broken?

No, it is not broken. Those boards only show scores from your friends, or from your country, so an empty one just means nobody in that group has set a score on that map yet.

## Do unranked / graveyard maps count?

Yes. On Torii, unranked, graveyard and loved maps give pp and have leaderboards. On official osu! standard those give no pp at all, so this is a real difference.

## Are Relax and Autopilot ranked?

Yes, both are fully ranked here, each with its own leaderboards.

## Can I use custom Double Time / Half Time rates?

Yes. Custom rate variants of DT, NC, HT, DC and Easy still earn pp on Torii, unlike official osu!.

## Did I get auto-banned?

No. Nothing on Torii auto-bans you and no score is auto-rejected. Every restriction is a manual staff decision. See [Restrictions & Appeals](/wiki/Restrictions).

## How do I appeal a restriction?

Open a ticket in the Discord and wait about a month before appealing. Details on the [Restrictions & Appeals](/wiki/Restrictions) page.

## I found a map giving crazy pp. It looks broken.

Report it in Discord instead of farming it. Because unranked maps give pp here, a broken map can hand out absurd pp, and knowingly farming one without reporting it is bannable.
