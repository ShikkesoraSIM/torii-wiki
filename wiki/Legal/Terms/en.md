## Points, cosmetics and donations

Torii points are made up. They are not money, they are not worth money, they cannot be cashed out, and you do not own them. The whole economy, every balance included, is reset before the public launch. The details are on the [Points & Economy](/wiki/Torii_points) page.

Donations pay for the hardware the server runs on. Donating gets you a supporter tag and cosmetic extras, and that is all it gets you: no points, no pp, no rank, no say in how a score is judged. A donation is a donation and not a purchase, so there is nothing to refund from our side. If a payment itself went wrong, that has to be sorted out with the payment provider.
